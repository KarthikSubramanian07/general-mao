# PRODUCT.md, General Mao

## What it is
Free online Mao: a shedding card game whose secret rules are invented and enforced
by an AI ("the General"). It never explains. You learn the rules only by breaking
them and being penalized. A table of strangers can play the real game blind,
together, refereed by something that never slips and never takes pity.

## Users
People who love deduction and social sabotage: the kind of person who inflicts Mao
on their friends. Often at night, often on a phone, often several to a room.

## Register
- **Landing / marketing (`/`):** brand. Design IS the pitch. Convey the premise and
  the menace; earn a click.
- **Table / lobby (`/t/:code`, `/play`):** product. Design SERVES the game. The tool
  disappears into the deduction.

## The one thing
Every decision protects two invariants at once: **the mystery** (rules never leak)
and **the fairness** (the General is lawful, never random). If a feature threatens
either, cut it.

## Voice
The General is deadpan, formal, judicial. It congratulates no one. It occasionally
says "Correct." and nothing else, which is worse. Copy is dry, exact, a little
menacing. No exclamation, no hype, no emoji.

## Anti-references (do not become these)
- Casino / poker-felt-and-gold glitz. This is a tribunal, not a card room.
- Generic dark developer-SaaS (Geist/Linear clone), warm-cream + terracotta AI default.
- Cheerful party-game bounce and confetti. The General does not celebrate.
- Editorial-serif magazine layout (display serif + italic + ruled columns).
